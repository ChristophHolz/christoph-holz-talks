A Keynote by Christoph Holz Sets the Tone for Your Event

Agentic Marketing Between Scale and Substance

When AI Agents Become Team Players in Customer Acquisition.

The speaker Christoph Holz affectionately calls his AI agent "Woody". For two years now, the autonomous AI has been answering customer inquiries faster than the competition. Other agents identify leads on the night shift and curate content from atomic text modules and CI-compliant image prompts. Only very rarely do they freak out. Holz shows what of this reliably delivers, where the limits lie and what happens when agents fail — and what elevates the role of the human. Whoever brings an agent onto the team is hiring someone: with onboarding, guardrails and a liability question. The algorithm multiplies — but it takes a human to experience. Entertaining, interactive and deliberately optimistic. Whoever understands how, wins.

Four Thoughts from the Talk

Marketing used to mean staying in people's minds. From now on, it means staying in the machine's model.

The best team of the future is mixed: one human, one agent—and neither does what the other does better.

Errors scale with computing power — ethics does not.

Creativity is always the improbable. And the improbable is precisely what the probability calculus of generative AI cannot handle.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and focal points are tailored to the industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, on customer acquisition, brand management and governance.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes expressly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes, and it's all about courage: vivid stories like the beloved agent "Woody" spark conversations that last well beyond the day. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that strengthens the brand.
