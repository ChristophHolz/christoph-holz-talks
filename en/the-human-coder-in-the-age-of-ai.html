Keynote · Christoph Holz

The Human Coder in the Age of AI

The future isn't programmed — it's trained.

Did I become a programmer to type code — or to solve problems? If coding gets democratized, who needs developers? Does off-the-shelf software have a future? Why is software engineering actually not engineering at all? Christoph Holz walks the audience through five thought experiments that remap software development, profession, and industry.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

The future is no longer shaped, it's coded. We nerds weren't prepared for that responsibility. We just wanted to play.

Human strength isn't calculating faster than the machine — it's knowing what's worth calculating.

Information technology is not technology. Technology is the supplement to physical labor. IT is the supplement to mental effort.

What Your Audience Takes Away from This Talk

Through vivid metaphors and examples, your guests grasp what artificial intelligence can do — and where its limits lie.
