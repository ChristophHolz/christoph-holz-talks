Marketing for Machines

Why Your Most Important Audience Will Soon No Longer Be Human

More and more people no longer ask Google but an AI: What should I buy? When the assistant makes the choice, marketing's most important audience shifts from the customer to the machine. At the same time, GenAI writes campaigns faster than any agency can brief them. Christoph Holz shows: that's exactly why brand becomes more important, not less — as the one thing the machine cannot hallucinate: trust. Three operational levers, five thought experiments. Those who understand how, win.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

Marketing used to mean staying in people's minds. From now on, it means staying in the machine's model.

It used to be the brand a human remembered that won. Soon it will be the brand a machine recommends.

Anyone who believes that neural AI sometimes hallucinates is wrong. It always hallucinates. But with a little luck, it sometimes happens to agree with reality.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and focal points are tailored to the industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to Answer Engine Optimization, neuromarketing, and AI assistants.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — courage, to be precise: vivid stories like the invisible bouncer of the AI answer spark conversations that last well beyond the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that strengthens your brand.
