When the Concrete Mixer Drives a Tesla

Artificial Intelligence and the Next Construction Economy

Order slumps, skills shortages, bureaucracy — and AI as a cure-all? Of course not. Entrepreneurs solve their own problems. Christoph Holz shows three levers that already work today — plus five thought experiments for the day after tomorrow, chosen by the audience: Does an autonomous truck need a cab? Do we want a self-driving 30-tonner rolling past a kindergarten? Autonomous machines and generative AI put cherished business models to the test. At the same time, more new business opportunities open up than ever before. Concrete, interactive, surprising. Those who understand how, win.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

Thought experiments are the training camp for a successful future.

Artificial intelligence doesn't replace craftsmanship — it liberates it.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and focal points are tailored to the industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to the skills shortage, prefabrication, and autonomous excavators.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — courage, to be precise: vivid stories like the self-driving 30-tonner outside the kindergarten spark conversations that outlast the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that empowers the construction sector.

Christoph Holz's Expertise

Expertise in AI in the construction industry

Christoph Holz knows the construction industry from the building site to the software: already as an HTL student he worked in metal fabrication, today he plays the sector digitally. For Bauwaren Mahler, holzweg built the building-materials-trade presence including warehouse and location mapping. Bringing him to the stage: the 100th-anniversary celebration of Würth Hochenburger, the leadership conference of Eiffage Infra-Bau, the BG BAU and the association of building-materials dealers. On stage, he answers the question of what happens when you cross a cement mixer with a Tesla — and what artificial intelligence sets in motion in an industry that's only just discovering its digital future.

Generative AI in Enterprise Use

On stage, Holz shows how companies turn ChatGPT, Anthropic & Co. into business success — and where the limits lie.

Expertise in Autonomous Mobility

Autonomous mobility follows Christoph Holz from the road all the way into the air: in 2014 he saw the welding robots at Tesla in Fremont, in 2026 the production of the G-Class at Magna Steyr. In Silicon Valley he found the autonomous Nuro delivery van parked at the curb and experienced the shock of his first autonomous cab ride in San Francisco; at ARX Robotics in Munich, the autonomous ground systems for Europe's security. On stage he answers the question of why the car is becoming an app — and the driver is moving to the home office.
