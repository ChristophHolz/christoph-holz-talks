Bullshit 4.0

Where the hell is the revolution in digitalization?

Picard's wallet, Walmart's Stone-Age database, and Winnetou as an executive coach: In "Bullshit 4.0," Christoph Holz separates the hype from what's really coming. The audience picks from a buffet of thought experiments — from AI to New Work to Digital Leadership — and joins the discussion live.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

Digitalization is not a form of industrialization, but its end.

Disruption is what established companies watch approach in slow motion — and consequently ignore.

There was never a 4th Industrial Revolution. Industry sounds so familiar, after all. Revolution — we've had that before. Giving a demon a familiar name does not tame it. Industry 4.0 is semantic self-deception.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and focal points are tailored to the industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to Artificial Intelligence, New Work and Digital Leadership.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — and that something is courage: vivid stories like Walmart's Stone Age database fuel conversations that last well beyond the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that strengthens the executive suite.
