A Keynote by Christoph Holz Sets the Tone for Your Event

10 years as a cyborg

The end of human evolution and its future. No transhumanism is no solution either.

For ten years, Christoph Holz has carried a chip under his skin. What began as a self-experiment leads straight into the philosophical questions about the development of Homo sapiens — now that its biological evolution is complete and the technological one is just beginning.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

What remains when AI takes over everything that can be optimized? The human being. With everything that cannot be optimized.

The digital future only counts if, in the end, it leaves more time for health, family, and everything that can't be digitized.

Every time we gain a new mode of thinking, we give something up. Humans traded photographic thinking for language. What will we trade for AI?

What Your Audience Takes Away from This Talk

Through vivid metaphors and examples, your guests grasp what technology can do — and where its limits lie.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and focal points are tailored to your industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to human enhancement, the merging of Man and Machine and digital ethics.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — courage, to be precise: vivid stories like the chip under the skin spark conversations that outlast the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that empowers people.
