What Robinson Crusoe knew about the home office

Perspectives for Organizations When the Office Is No Longer the Center

Did we introduce home office to cut costs — or to finally lead differently? Is it too much to ask AI to hold our organization together when no one sits in the office anymore? Robinson had to reorganize his island first, too.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

Management is the decoupling of power and morality.

When all inhuman work is digitalized away, what remains is the human being.

Location as an efficiency trap: The office of the future is no longer an office.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and key topics are tailored to the industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to New Work, the skills shortage, and AI.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — courage, to be precise: vivid stories like the campfire on the beach spark conversations that last well beyond the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that strengthens your organization.

Autonomous Mobility
