Education is relationship work

How AI Delivers on the Educational Ideal Wilhelm von Humboldt Once Promised Us

Are kids getting dumber in the age of AI? Quite the opposite. Wilhelm von Humboldt invented the modern education system. He didn't want students who knew the answers — he wanted people who asked the questions. Personal growth, individual learning paths, relationships instead of a class-period-subject grind. His ideal went unfulfilled for 200 years — buried under an industrial mass education that shaped children into cadets, workers, and consumers. Artificial intelligence is finally redeeming it — and turning education into what it was always meant to be: relationship work.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

Education was never about transferring knowledge, but about relationship. When the machine takes over the knowledge, there is finally room for what only humans can do.

For 200 years, the humanist ideal of education was a promise without a budget. AI is the first private tutor every child can afford.

It is not children who grow dumber in the age of AI — it is the rhythm of grade, lesson, and subject, which standardized their curiosity, that finally becomes obsolete.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and focal points are tailored to the industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to individual learning paths, the AI tutor, and relationship work.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Yes — courage, to be precise: vivid stories like the truck on the pedestrian bridge spark conversations that outlast the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that empowers our children.
