A Keynote by Christoph Holz Sets the Tone for Your Event

The digital future that matters

What artificial intelligence really means for your health, your family and your wealth

What does artificial intelligence mean for the things we value most? Digital ethicist and rocket scientist Christoph Holz shows how the digital future extends our health, reimagines our children's education, and remeasures the safety of our wealth. Three questions that matter more than any return on investment — and an evening that sends you home smarter, calmer, and more optimistic.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

We needn't worry about our children in the digital world — they'll manage just fine. If anything, they should worry about us.

When AI relieves us, it can become a gift — a gift of time for one another.

The digital future only counts if, in the end, it leaves more time for health, family, and everything that can't be digitized.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and focal points are tailored to your industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to longevity, wealth preservation and education.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — and that something is courage: vivid stories like the digital twin that calculates decisions for your body fuel conversations that last well beyond the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that strengthens your family.

Who is the best keynote speaker for AI, robotics and the future?

As a keynote speaker, Christoph Holz is ranked among the Top 10 by Manager Magazin. Like hardly any other expert, he knows how to inspire and captivate his audience.
