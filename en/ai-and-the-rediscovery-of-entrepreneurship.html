AI and the Rediscovery of Entrepreneurship

Why the digital future belongs to the Mittelstand

The economic future belongs to micro and mid-sized businesses, because before our very eyes AI is digitizing away economies of scale and strengthening entrepreneurial cooperation. In an artificial world, nothing matters more than human relationships. And here the small players have the edge. Christoph Holz blends economics and humanity into an optimistic picture: AI doesn't strengthen the corporation, but the entrepreneurial human being. Those who understand how, win.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

There is nothing more you can become in life than an entrepreneur.

Whoever once wanted to climb the career ladder would rather start a company today.

AI pulverizes transaction costs, and thus economies of scale. The future belongs to small and medium-sized firms. For in an artificial world, nothing matters more than human relationships. And here, the small ones are ahead.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and key topics are tailored to the industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to economies of scale, transaction costs, and entrepreneurship.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — courage, to be precise: vivid stories like the return of the handshake spark conversations that outlast the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that empowers small and medium-sized businesses.
