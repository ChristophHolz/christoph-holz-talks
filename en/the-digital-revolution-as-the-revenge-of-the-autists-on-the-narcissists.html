A Keynote by Christoph Holz Sets the Tone for Your Event

The digital revolution as the revenge of the autists on the narcissists

Why systematizing intelligence and neurodiversity become the decisive edge in the age of AI

For decades they were laughed at — the nerds, the oddballs, the contrarians in the computer basement. Today they program the future. The digital revolution is the revenge of the autists on the narcissists. In a world being permeated by artificial intelligence, the very traits long dismissed as deficits become an advantage: systematic intelligence, focus on detail, neurodiversity. A wordplay-rich case for being different — with three thought experiments and an invitation to see cognitive diversity as a competitive edge. Those who understand how, win.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

Systematic intelligence. Rules and patterns. A love of detail and savant talent. The cultural skill of the future.

The future is no longer shaped, it's coded. We nerds weren't prepared for that responsibility. We just wanted to play.

Digitalization is not technology. Digitalization is the emancipation movement of the nerds. We turned our discrimination into world domination. Now we're in a real mess.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and focal points are tailored to your industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to neurodiversity, cognitive diversity and systematic intelligence.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — and that something is courage: vivid stories like the flat-surface Tetris at the supermarket checkout fuel conversations that last well beyond the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that strengthens your team.
