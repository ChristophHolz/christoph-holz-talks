DINNER TALK: The Terminator Arrives via Amazon

A Witty Dinner Speech on AI, Robots, and the End of the World

Cyborg, rocket scientist and digital ethicist Christoph Holz takes your guests on an entertaining journey into the age of AI — sharp, optimistic and guaranteed free of PowerPoint fatigue. It's long been too late for pessimism anyway.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

Should Terminators really come, there will be good ones too — from Austria.

Housework is a common cause of breakups. A good household robot will save relationships.

By the way, when it comes to AI wiping out humanity, I don't believe in combat robots. We'd notice those. No, the extermination will be handled by sex droids. Going extinct has never been as pleasant as it is today.

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and key topics are tailored to the industry in advance with the organizer — here, for instance, to Artificial Intelligence, robots, and quantum computers.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — courage, to be precise: vivid stories like the quantum computer in the wine cellar spark conversations that outlast the evening. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that empowers the guests.
