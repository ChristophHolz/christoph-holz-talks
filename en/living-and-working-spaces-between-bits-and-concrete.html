Living and Working Spaces Between Bits and Concrete

Thought Experiments on the Future of Real Estate and AI.

What does Artificial Intelligence mean for the real estate industry of tomorrow? Christoph Holz — digital ethicist, graduate computer scientist (Dipl.-Inform.) and TV expert — draws the arc from concrete to artificial intelligence: what AI can really do around buildings, marketing and management, where its limits lie, and why location is being reinvented right now. Using vivid examples from Zurich's future districts to Silicon Valley, he shows how spaces become experiences and what remains when the office of the future is no longer an office — entertaining, interactive and deliberately optimistic. Those who understand how, win.

Three Thoughts from the Talk

Location as an efficiency trap: The office of the future is no longer an office.

Why do we even still need cities when everyone has a smartphone today?

In the 18th century, weavers left their home office to go to work. Only in the factory did the steam engine deliver the torque needed for the great looms. But steam engines have been retired. So why are there still factories?

Questions and Answers about the Talk

Does the talk address the specific needs of the audience?

Yes: examples and focus areas are tailored to the industry with the organizer in advance — here, for instance, to PropTech, land use and the future of office and retail.

Are participants met where they are?

Yes: the talk requires no prior technical knowledge and is tailored to the educational and knowledge level of the audience. Usually Holz joins in beforehand to get to know his listeners.

Is the talk interactive?

Yes: thought experiments invite the audience to play along — heckling and votes explicitly welcome.

Does anything from the talk stick — and does it inspire courage or fear?

Yes — and it's about courage: vivid stories like Zurich's Hunziker Areal spark conversation long after the evening ends. Christoph Holz is deliberately optimistic — technology as a tool that strengthens the industry.

Christoph Holz's Expertise

Forms of Living

Christoph Holz knows new forms of living first-hand: he visited Switzerland's flagship cooperative projects — the Kalkbreite cooperative and the Hunziker Areal "Mehr als Wohnen" in Zurich, as well as Kraftwerk1 Zwicky Süd, where mixed-use developments reconnect living, working and community in new ways. On stage, he shows how digitalization and Artificial Intelligence are shaping neighborhoods, sharing models and the way we'll live together tomorrow.

TV expert

Christoph Holz is a sought-after TV expert on digitalization, AI and the future — whether in the studio or from his private home film studio — appearing on RTL, ServusTV, ProSieben, Sat.1, n-tv, WELT, Deutsche Welle, Hamburg1, ORF and CNBC, among others.

Lectureship in Digital Ethics, University of Applied Sciences

As a lecturer, Holz teaches Digital Ethics to aspiring engineers at the University of Applied Sciences Upper Austria. Ethics must not moralize with a wagging finger. As Ethics by Design, it has to be part of the development process from the very start.
